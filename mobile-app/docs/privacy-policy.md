# Royal SMM mobile app privacy summary

Royal SMM is a native Android shell for `https://royal.t20tech.site`. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not collect information beyond what the Royal SMM website collects during account use.

The app stores WebView cookies, local storage, and session data on the device so that the website can keep a signed-in session between launches. These values are excluded from Android cloud backup. Files selected through the website are passed to the website only for the action the customer started. Downloads are saved by Android DownloadManager.

The app may open WhatsApp, phone, email, SMS, mobile-money, payment, or other links in the relevant installed system app. Those apps process data under their own privacy policies. The app requests network access and network-state access. Notification permission is reserved for the optional Firebase notification feature and is not requested until that feature is configured.

For account deletion, payment questions, or data requests, contact the Royal SMM operator through the support channel published on the website.
